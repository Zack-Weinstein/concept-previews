The shop

A family shop on 13th Street.

Scrooge is husband-and-wife run. Reviews often mention Mason on the food and Camila on the hand-whisked matcha. They bake the baguettes here, make the fruit purées from organic fruit, and use real ingredients — the shop’s own words.

The dining room was renovated to add more seating, so you can eat in when you want to stay a while. Woman-owned, family-owned, and a short walk from campus.
