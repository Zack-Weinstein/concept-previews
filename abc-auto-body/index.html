About the shop

A Boulder family shop, not a chain.

ABC Auto Body of Boulder has been serving this city since 1974. It is family-owned and operated, with personal service, fair prices, and honest work meant to bring people back — or send their friends.

Technicians work on all makes and models. Paint is matched to the manufacturer color code, and the team is I-CAR certified: trained through the international nonprofit that sets standards for complete, safe collision repairs.

Walk-in estimates are welcome Monday through Thursday. Friday estimates are by appointment.
