Spookadelia: Unspoken

The eighth installment of the Spookadelia series. On this self-guided journey, guests set out to save Gilly's voice, protect the prism portals, and banish Doubt and Shadow back to the echo.

Collect all 13 tokens to complete the mission. It's spooky, but family-friendly and full of delight, and guests who finish get special treats at the end.

FAQ

Good to know

Where is Spectra Art Space?

1836 S Broadway in Denver, Colorado, on South Broadway's "Green Mile."

Is Spookadelia kid-friendly?

Yes. Spookadelia: Unspoken is an all-ages experience. It's "spooky," but family-friendly and full of delight.

Do I need to be 21 for glass blowing?

Yes. Glass blowing lessons are 21+ and you'll need a valid ID when you arrive. Call (720) 535-7244 or email us to schedule after you buy your pass.

Can I rent the space for a private event?

Yes. The gallery holds about 122 people inside and 125 in the courtyard (247 combined), with pricing starting at $100. Email spectraartdenver@gmail.com to book.
