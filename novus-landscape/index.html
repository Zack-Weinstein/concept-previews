FAQ

Common questions

Where does Novus Landscape work?

We're based in Steamboat Springs, Colorado and serve Steamboat Springs, Hayden, Craig, and the surrounding areas of Routt and Moffat Counties.

What services do you offer?

Landscape design, construction, and maintenance: garden design, hardscapes (patios, pavers, flagstone, retaining walls), outdoor kitchens and firepits, water features, landscape lighting, lawn care, sod, tree and shrub planting, sprinkler service, and seasonal clean-ups.

How long has Novus been in business?

Our team has more than 25 years of experience designing, building, and maintaining properties in the Steamboat area.

What are your hours, and how do I pay?

We're open Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with emergency services available. We accept cash, check, ACH, and wire transfer.
