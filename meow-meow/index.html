The shop

A curated mix, picked by the owner, on 13th Street.

The Hill’s directory calls Meow Meow a quirky retailer with a long history here. The storefront is at 1118 13th Street, a short walk from campus, packed with clothing, accessories, jewelry, gifts, sunglasses, books, cards, and hats.

Come wander

Built for browsing.

This is a shop you spend time in. Reviews often mention the card selection right as you walk in, one-of-a-kind jewelry by local Colorado makers, and clothes you will not find in a mall.

If you are stumped on a gift, this is a good first stop on The Hill.
